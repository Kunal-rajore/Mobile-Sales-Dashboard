1. Mobile Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to visualize and analyze mobile sales performance across India — covering total revenue, transaction trends, customer satisfaction, and payment preferences by city, brand, and model.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Mobile Sales Dashboard provides a visually dynamic and data-driven analysis of mobile sales across different brands, models, cities, and time periods.
It enables sales teams, analysts, and management to monitor performance, identify high-performing products, and optimize regional marketing and sales strategies.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform for visualization and dashboard design.

🔄 Power Query Editor – Used for cleaning, merging, and transforming raw data.

🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Created calculated measures and KPIs for insights.

🗂️ Data Modeling – Established relationships between sales, customers, and transaction tables.

📁 File Format – .pbix for Power BI reports and .png for visual previews.

4. Data Source

Source: Sample dataset simulating mobile brand sales data across Indian cities.
The dataset includes the following details:

City and Region

Mobile Brand and Model

Total Sales, Quantity, and Transactions

Payment Method (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Cash)

Customer Ratings and Feedback

5. Features / Highlights
5.1 Business Problem

The mobile retail division required a centralized visualization to analyze sales data across India. Traditional static reports made it difficult to identify city-wise performance, sales trends, and customer behavior.

5.2 Goal of the Dashboard

To design a comprehensive sales analytics tool that:

Tracks major KPIs such as Total Sales, Quantity, Transactions, and Average Order Value.

Analyzes sales trends by city, brand, and month.

Highlights best-selling products and payment preferences.

Measures customer satisfaction and purchasing behavior.

5.3 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Top KPIs (Header Section):
Displays real-time metrics:

💰 Total Sales: ₹769M

📦 Total Quantity: 19K

🧾 Transactions: 4K

📈 Average Value: ₹40K

Total Sales by City (Map Visual):
Interactive India map showing top-performing cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, and Hyderabad with bubble size representing sales volume.

Total Quantity by Month (Line Chart):
Illustrates monthly sales quantity trends, helping to identify peak and low-performing months.

Customer Ratings (Bar Chart):
Displays the rating distribution, showcasing a strong base of satisfied customers.

Transactions by Payment Method (Pie Chart):
Breaks down the usage of different payment options —
UPI (26%), Debit Card (25%), Credit Card (25%), and Cash (24%) — showing the steady shift toward digital payments.

Total Sales by Mobile Model (Bar Chart):
Compares performance of top models such as iPhone SE, OnePlus, and Galaxy Note.

Sales by Brand (Table Visual):
Summarizes sales performance by brand (Apple, Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi), including total sales, quantity sold, and transactions.

Total Sales by Day Name (Area Chart):
Highlights daily sales trends, revealing Saturday and Sunday as the most profitable days.

5.4 Business Impact & Insights

Performance Monitoring: Identifies high-performing brands, cities, and models.

Strategic Planning: Guides decision-making for regional marketing and inventory allocation.

Customer Insights: Reveals preferences for payment methods and product ratings.

Revenue Optimization: Supports sales forecasting and business growth planning.
